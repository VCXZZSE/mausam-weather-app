# Mausam Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 15 September 2026  
**Publisher:** Oryza  
**Contact:** repome_maiml2024@msit.edu.in

Mausam collects only the information needed to provide weather, environmental information and personalized recommendations.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Profile information:** You may provide your name, age, height, weight, gender preference, weather sensitivities, health considerations, goals and activity level.

**Why:** To personalize weather briefings, activity recommendations and health-conscious guidance. This information is not used to diagnose or treat medical conditions.

**Location:** Mausam may use your device latitude/longitude, location accuracy, manually selected location, locality, state/region, country, PIN code and time zone.

**Why:** To provide weather for your area, identify the location, calculate local time and provide location-specific guidance. You may use manual location selection instead of device location.

**Weather and environmental data:** Mausam retrieves weather forecasts, temperature, rainfall, humidity, wind, UV, AQI, pollen estimates, astronomy information, outdoor guidance and official advisories for the selected location.

**Technical information:** Our infrastructure or service providers may receive IP address, device/browser/OS information, timestamps, request details, error information and security/server logs needed to operate and protect the service. Mausam does not use this information for advertising profiles.

## 2. Device Permissions

Mausam may request **location permission** only when you choose the current-location feature.

The Android app requires **Internet access** to retrieve weather, location, air-quality, advisory and related information.

Mausam does not intentionally request access to contacts, photos/media, camera, microphone, SMS, phone calls, calendar, Bluetooth or advertising ID in the current implementation.

## 3. Data Stored on Your Device

Mausam stores your profile, selected location and theme preference in local browser/WebView storage so the app can restore them when reopened.

Logging out clears the saved profile and location. Clearing app/browser data or uninstalling the app also removes local storage.

Removing local data does not necessarily remove information already processed by external services or included in server logs.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide and restore your profile and selected location
- Retrieve weather, AQI and related environmental information
- Provide personalized recommendations and briefings
- Display local time and other location-based information
- Maintain, secure, troubleshoot and improve Mausam
- Detect abuse, fraud and security incidents
- Comply with applicable law

Mausam does not use profile information for medical diagnosis or treatment.

## 5. Services We Use

To provide Mausam's features, selected information may be processed by our backend and service providers, including weather, geocoding, air-quality, astronomy and advisory services.

Depending on the feature, this may include your location, locality, coordinates, selected search text, PIN code, derived persona or sensitivity level and normal technical request information.

For example, location searches may use Mausam services or **OpenStreetMap Nominatim**. Third-party providers process information according to their own policies.

The current personalized-briefing request does **not** send your name, age, height, weight, gender or complete raw profile to that endpoint; it uses derived information such as persona/sensitivity and location data.

## 6. Retention

Profile, location and theme data remain on your device until you remove them.

Weather and location requests may be temporarily cached by Mausam, its infrastructure or providers to improve performance and reliability.

Server and third-party logs may be retained according to applicable security, legal and operational requirements.

Mausam currently does not provide a user account or dashboard for directly deleting server logs. Privacy requests can be sent to the contact email above.

## 7. Sharing and Disclosure

Mausam **does not sell personal information**.

Information may be shared with service providers required to operate Mausam, relevant weather/location/environmental services, or where required by law or necessary to protect users, the service or others.

Information may also be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition or transfer of the service, subject to applicable law.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information. However, no Internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal information, withdraw location permission and raise privacy complaints.

You can deny or revoke location permission through your device settings and use manual location selection where available.

Contact **repome_maiml2024@msit.edu.in** for privacy requests.

## 10. Children

Mausam is not knowingly directed to children below the minimum age required by applicable law. The publisher will define the applicable age and child-data process before public release.

## 11. International Processing

Mausam or its service providers may process information in countries outside your country of use. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be applied.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Mausam's features, providers, data practices or legal requirements change. Material changes will be communicated where required.
