# Mausam — Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. What is Mausam?
Mausam is a personalized weather application that combines weather, air quality, environmental information and location-based recommendations to help you understand what the weather means for you.

### 2. Why does Mausam need my location?
Your location helps Mausam provide weather, AQI, alerts, local time and recommendations for your selected area.

You can also search for a location manually without granting device location access.

### 3. Do I have to share my location?
No. Location permission is optional. You can use manual location search instead.

### 4. What personal information does Mausam ask for?
Depending on the features you use, Mausam may ask for information such as your name, age, height, weight, gender preference, weather sensitivities, health considerations, goals and activity level.

These details are used to personalize your weather experience.

### 5. Why does Mausam ask about my health or sensitivities?
This helps Mausam provide more relevant weather and outdoor guidance—for example, information related to heat, UV, air quality, humidity or other environmental conditions.

Mausam does not diagnose or treat medical conditions.

### 6. Is my health information shared?
Mausam's current personalized-briefing request does not send your complete raw profile, such as your name, age, height or weight, to the briefing endpoint. Only relevant derived information and location data may be used for personalization.

### 7. Does Mausam track me continuously?
No. Mausam requests location when you use the current-location feature. You can revoke location permission through your device settings.

### 8. Does Mausam sell my data?
No. Mausam does not sell personal information.

Some information may be processed by service providers needed to provide weather, location, air-quality and other app features.

### 9. What information is stored on my device?
Mausam stores your profile, selected location and theme preference locally so the app can restore them when you return.

### 10. Can I delete my information?
You can remove locally stored profile and location information by logging out, clearing the app/browser data or uninstalling the app.

Information already processed by external services or included in server logs may not be removed automatically.

### 11. Where does Mausam get its weather information?
Mausam retrieves weather and environmental information from configured weather, air-quality, geocoding, astronomy and advisory services, which may include official sources.

### 12. Are Mausam's recommendations medical advice?
No. Mausam provides general weather and environmental guidance. It should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice.

### 13. Does Mausam work without location permission?
Yes. You can manually search and select a location where the feature is available.

### 14. What permissions does the app require?
Mausam currently requires Internet access and may request location permission when you choose the current-location feature.

It does not intentionally request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, SMS, phone calls or calendar.

### 15. Can I change my preferences later?
Yes. Your selected profile and preferences can be changed through the available profile/settings options in the app.

### 16. Is my data secure?
Mausam uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

### 17. Can I use Mausam without creating an account?
Yes. The current version does not require a Mausam account or password.
